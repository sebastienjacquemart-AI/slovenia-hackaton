Why this forecast?

Every forecast starts from a plain average, then gets adjusted up or down based on things like recent sales, promotions, and the calendar. Each bar below is one day — red means something pushed the number up, blue means something pushed it down. The bar heights are on the model's own internal scale (use them to see which factor matters most, not as a literal unit count) — the actual forecast in units is shown below the chart.
